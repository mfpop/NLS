# DOMAIN GLOSSARY

## Plant
Factory location.

## Department
Functional area.

## ResourceGroup
Group of similar resources.

## Resource
Machine or workstation.

## ProcessFlow
Product routing.

## ProcessStep
Single operation.

## StandardWork
Approved working method.

## ProductionCycle
Actual execution record.

## DowntimeEvent
Machine stoppage.

## QualityEvent
Defect record.

## Kaizen
Improvement action.

## GembaWalk
Shop-floor observation.
