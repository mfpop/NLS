# Profile Page – User Guide (LeanSynk)

## Purpose

This page centralizes your professional identity used across the platform (Gemba Walk, Control Tower, Dashboard).

It ensures:

- Clear ownership
- Consistent structure
- Readable experience history

---

## Page Structure

### 1. Header

- **Avatar** → Click to upload/change profile image
- **Full Name** → Main identifier
- **Role Title** → Current position
- **Completion Bar** → Profile completeness indicator
- **Completion Tags**:
  - Identity
  - Contact
  - Location
  - Summary
  - Experience
  - Education
- **Edit Profile Button** → Enables editing

---

### 2. Personal Information

Core identity and structure:

- First Name
- Last Name
- Email (required, valid format)
- Phone (international format recommended)
- Full Address (City, State, Country)
- Role
- Reports To (optional)
- Plant (from system)
- Department (from system)
- Languages

### Rules

- Name, Email, Role are mandatory
- Use real system structure (Plant / Department)
- Avoid placeholders or fake data

---

### 3. Professional Summary

Short description of your scope and strengths.

### Structure

- 2–3 short sentences OR bullet-style text
- Focus on:
  - Scope (plants, teams, systems)
  - Strengths (lean, digital, leadership)
  - Type of work (operations, transformation, support)

### Example

- Operations leader focused on lean transformation and cross-plant standardization.
- Experienced in deploying KPI routines and stabilizing bottlenecks.

### Rules

- Max ~300 characters
- Avoid generic phrases
- Be specific and operational

---

### 4. Work History

Professional experience in reverse chronological order.

Each entry includes:

- Role Title
- Company Name
- Period (Start – End / Present)
- Description (short, structured)

### Description Rules (STRICT)

- Max 2–3 short statements per role
- Each statement:
  - Starts with action verb (Led, Improved, Implemented, Reduced)
  - Minimum 6 words
  - Ends with period
- No duplicate meaning
- No long paragraphs

### Example

- Rolled out standard metrics and accountability routines.
- Improved production stability through better scheduling discipline.

---

### 5. Education

Formal education only.

- Degree
- Institution
- Period

### Rules

- Keep concise
- Only relevant education
- No descriptions needed

---

## Completion Logic (UI)

Profile completion is based on:

- Identity filled
- Contact filled
- Location filled
- Summary present
- At least 1 Work Experience
- At least 1 Education entry

### Completion States

- **100%** → Complete profile
- **<100%** → Missing sections highlighted

---

## Highlights (UI – Header Tags)

Displayed as completion tags:

- Identity
- Contact
- Location
- Summary
- Experience
- Education

### Rules

- Tags reflect completeness only
- No KPI or scoring shown here

---

## Best Practices

- Keep content short and structured
- Use action-oriented language
- Maintain consistency across roles
- Keep profile aligned with real responsibilities
- Avoid unnecessary details

---

## What NOT to Do

- Do not leave key fields empty
- Do not write long paragraphs
- Do not duplicate information across roles
- Do not use vague statements (e.g., "worked on improvements")
- Do not add metrics/KPIs in profile (handled elsewhere)

---

## Result

A complete profile should:

- Clearly show role and experience
- Be easy to scan (<2 seconds)
- Follow consistent structure
- Support operational clarity across the app

---

**This page feeds multiple modules → keep it clean, structured, and accurate.**
